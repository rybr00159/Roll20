# Someone Must Hurt — GM reference

Converted from the supplied Level 8 statblocks for five 8th-level PCs, using 2014 5e. Keep this handout private: it contains encounter mechanics and spoilers. It supplements the four NPC imports.

## Living Chamber

Add a separate **Living Chamber** entry at **initiative count 20, losing initiative ties**. It uses one lair action per round and cannot use the same option two rounds in succession. Track the last option manually.

These are covenant effects. They continue while at least one conduit remains active, even if Yselle is incapacitated or defeated, and do not require Yselle to see the target.

**Pain Pulse.** Choose one creature within 30 feet of an active conduit. It makes a **DC 16 Constitution save**, taking **10 (3d6) necrotic damage** on failure or half on success.

**Grasping Flesh.** Choose one creature touching the chamber floor. It makes a **DC 16 Dexterity save** or is restrained until initiative count 20 of the next round. A creature can use an action to make a **DC 16 Strength (Athletics) check**, freeing itself or a creature within its reach on success. All existing Grasping Flesh restraints also end if all three conduits are disabled.

Make ordinary object tokens and use an **unlinked HP bar for each**. The heart's HP are separate from Nera; do not use the heart as a general damage pool for Transfer Injury. The source gives no physical sizes for these objects.

### Disable a conduit

Reduce it to **0 HP**, or use an **action within 5 feet** to succeed on one of these checks:

- **DC 16 Intelligence (Arcana)**
- **DC 16 Wisdom (Medicine)**
- **DC 16 Intelligence (Religion)**

A failed check deals **7 (2d6) necrotic damage** to the creature attempting it. Another attempt is allowed on a later turn. A conduit disabled by damage or a successful skill check stays disabled until the covenant is resolved; do not reactivate it on an ordinary round timer.

### Dispel Magic exception

For this adventure specifically, **Dispel Magic automatically disables a targeted conduit for 1 minute**. It requires no ability check. Track the temporary disable separately from a damage/check disable: when the minute ends, the conduit reactivates if the covenant is unresolved and it has not otherwise been disabled.

The conduits are **objects**. A spell restricted to creature targets cannot target them unless its own rules allow it. This special Dispel Magic interaction does not turn them into creatures or make all of Yselle's features spells.

### When all three are disabled

- Stop the chamber's lair actions and end existing **Grasping Flesh** restraints.
- Suspend Yselle's **Covenant Regeneration** and access to **Transfer Injury** while all three stay disabled. Her other features still function. This does not automatically end her separate **Living Suture** restraints.
- Make the suspended heart vulnerable with the AC, HP and immunities above. Lower its ribcage toward the pit's rim as the final conduit shuts down, so characters can reach it for the replacement-vessel ending.
- If a temporarily dispelled conduit reactivates, lair actions and Yselle's conduit-dependent features become available again. Track whether any other conduit remains disabled by damage or a check.

Disabling the conduits alone neither breaks the pact nor returns injuries to the village. Resolve the three endings using the original adventure's rules. Those ending rules are not supplied in the uploaded statblocks and are not invented here.

The upload does not say whether the heart becomes protected again if a temporarily dispelled conduit reactivates after the heart was exposed. Use any clarification in the original adventure, or make that ruling at the table; these imports do not automate it.

## Yselle's combat reminders

Both portraits use **AC 17, HP 170, one continuous HP pool**. Her combat appearance can be revealed when negotiations fail. The later scream is **The Unfolding**, not another HP pool.

- **Regeneration:** +5 HP at the **end** of her turn, only if she has at least 1 HP and an active conduit remains.
- **The Unfolding:** first reduction to **85 HP or fewer** while not incapacitated; chosen creatures within **30 feet** that can hear her save **Wisdom DC 16** or are frightened until the end of their own next turn. Mark it used after the trigger.
- **Living Suture:** recharge **5–6**. One visible creature within **60 feet** saves **Dexterity DC 16**; failure deals **3d6 piercing** and restrains for **1 minute**. An action with **Athletics DC 16** can free oneself or a creature within reach. The sutures can be destroyed instead. No repeating saving throw is granted by the supplied feature.
- **Multiattack:** two Bone Scalpel attacks; replace one attack with Living Suture when available.
- **Transfer Injury:** reaction, **3/day**, active conduit required. Reduce incoming damage by **10**, minimum 0. Describe Nera's corresponding wound without tracking Nera's HP.
- **Legendary Resistance:** **1/day**.
- **Legendary actions:** **2**, restored at the start of her turn. At the end of another creature's turn use one option: **Surgical Step** costs 1 and moves up to **15 feet** without opportunity attacks; **Bone Scalpel** costs 2 and makes one attack.
- **Bone Scalpel:** **+8**, **10-foot reach**, **2d8+4 slashing plus 1d6 necrotic**.
- **Necrotic resistance** applies to Yselle. Her listed features are monster features, not spells; she has no Spellcasting trait.

## Other monsters

**Suture Attendants:** living humanoids, not undead. Each has **60 HP**. On needle hits, Living Sutures can grapple a Medium or smaller target **once per turn**, escape **DC 14**, maximum one target held by that attendant. At 0 HP, trigger Death Spasm: all creatures within **5 feet**, **Dexterity DC 13**, **2d6 piercing on failure only**. They can be deceived, persuaded, charmed or spared. Their opening masks can reveal their former lives.

**Many-Handed Crawler:** **102 HP**. Multiattack makes two hand attacks and one beak attack. A hand hit can grapple a Medium or smaller creature, **escape DC 15**; it is also restrained. Only one held creature at a time. Its hands reach **10 feet**, but the bite reaches only **5 feet**, so it must move or drag a distant held target before biting. Mimicry is recognized with **Wisdom (Insight) DC 14**.

**Spinal Crawler:** **110 HP**. Multiattack makes two claw attacks and one beak attack. A Medium or smaller creature hit by a claw saves **Strength DC 15** or falls prone. Within **5 feet**, a subsequent bite can benefit from prone advantage; a melee attack made from 10 feet does not gain that advantage. The faded anchor tattoo is Cale's clue only if the matching tattoo was established earlier.

Both crawlers have **blindsight 30 feet and are blind beyond that radius**, and Spider Climb. They use their distinct imported stat blocks, replacing the earlier ordinary-grick suggestion. The mirror encounter can be bypassed using the reincorporation order and trolley described in the original adventure.

2014 party thresholds: **2,250 easy; 4,500 medium; 7,000 hard; 10,500 deadly**. These use the source's encounter multipliers; conduits and ritual actions add pressure beyond those XP values. CRs are homebrew design estimates, not playtest results.

The sixth bell retains the original **eight-round deadline**, counted from the party's entry into the final chamber. Track the round count separately from initiative and from each temporary conduit disable. Allow the module's offered short rest with its **one-bell cost**. Use the stated finale counts; make Yselle's dependence on the conduits visible to the players.

**Nera** is the ritual captive. **The Listener** is represented in combat by the chamber's lair actions, not a conventional sky target. Neither requires an invented creature stat block. Other NPCs, Mercy Marks, recruitment, sharing rituals and endings retain the original adventure's rules. The optional well encounter is outside this uploaded encounter set.
