# Someone Must Hurt — Level 8 Monster Statblocks
*For five 8th-level characters. D&D 5e, 2014 rules.*

# Suture Attendant
*Medium humanoid (human), lawful neutral*

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 60 (8d8 + 24)
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 7 (-2) | 10 (+0) | 5 (-3) |

**Saving Throws** Con +5
**Skills** Athletics +5
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
**Languages** Common (can't speak while its mask is sealed)
**Challenge** 2 (450 XP)

***Living Sutures.*** Once per turn, when the attendant hits a Medium or smaller creature with a Surgical Needle attack, it can grapple the target (escape DC 14). The attendant can grapple only one creature at a time.

***Death Spasm.*** When the attendant is reduced to 0 hit points, its sutures burst. Each creature within 5 feet of it must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or take 7 (2d6) piercing damage.

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** The attendant makes two Surgical Needle attacks.

***Surgical Needle.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage.

**DM note:** Use the same statistics for both Suture Attendant token portraits. These altered people can still be deceived, persuaded, charmed, or spared; they are not mindless undead. When one falls, its mask can open long enough for it to say, ‘I had a family.’

# The Rejected: Many-Handed Crawler
*Medium aberration, unaligned*

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 102 (12d8 + 48)
**Speed** 30 ft., climb 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18 (+4) | 14 (+2) | 18 (+4) | 3 (-4) | 12 (+1) | 5 (-3) |

**Skills** Athletics +7, Stealth +5
**Senses** blindsight 30 ft. (blind beyond this radius), passive Perception 11
**Languages** understands Common but can speak only by using its Mimicry trait
**Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP)

***Spider Climb.*** The crawler can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

***Mimicry.*** The crawler can mimic short phrases and other sounds it has heard. A creature that hears the sounds can tell they are imitations with a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check.

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** The crawler makes two Grasping Hand attacks and one Bone Beak attack.

***Grasping Hand.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, the crawler can grapple it (escape DC 15). Until this grapple ends, the target is restrained. The crawler can grapple only one creature at a time.

***Bone Beak.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) piercing damage.

**DM note:** This is the grasping, coiled portrait. It tries to seize a character, then bite the held target while its partner attacks exposed adventurers. A creature grappled 10 feet away is outside the beak's reach; the crawler must close the distance or drag it closer before biting it. Mimicry is a horror cue, not evidence of a genuine alternate timeline.

## Actions

***Hooked Claw.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

***Bone Beak.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) piercing damage.

**DM note:** This is the arching, spine-backed portrait with the faded anchor tattoo. It closes to within 5 feet before attacking when possible, knocks a target prone with its claws, and bites it with advantage. If using the tattoo as Cale's clue, establish the matching tattoo on Cale earlier; otherwise it belonged to another patient. The two Rejected are distinct creatures for this encounter, replacing the earlier suggestion to use ordinary grick statistics.

# Matron Yselle, the Anatomist of Mercy
*Medium aberration, lawful evil*

**Armor Class** 17 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 170 (20d8 + 80)
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18 (+4) | 14 (+2) | 18 (+4) | 18 (+4) | 16 (+3) | 16 (+3) |

**Saving Throws** Con +8, Wis +7
**Skills** Deception +7, Insight +7, Medicine +11, Perception +7, Religion +8
**Damage Resistances** necrotic
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 17
**Languages** Common, Celestial, Deep Speech
**Challenge** 10 (5,900 XP)

***Legendary Resistance (1/Day).*** If Yselle fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.

***Covenant Regeneration.*** Yselle regains 5 hit points at the end of her turn if she has at least 1 hit point and at least one conduit in the living chamber remains active.

***The Unfolding.*** The first time Yselle is reduced to 85 hit points or fewer without being incapacitated, her second mouth screams. Each creature of her choice within 30 feet of her that can hear her must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened of her until the end of its next turn.

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** Yselle makes two Bone Scalpel attacks. She can use Living Suture, if available, in place of one attack.

***Bone Scalpel.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) necrotic damage.

***Living Suture (Recharge 5–6).*** Yselle targets one creature she can see within 60 feet of her. The target must succeed on a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw or take 10 (3d6) piercing damage and be restrained by magical sutures for 1 minute. A creature can use its action to make a DC 16 Strength (Athletics) check, freeing itself or a creature within its reach on a success. The sutures can also be attacked and destroyed (AC 13; 15 hit points; immunity to poison and psychic damage). Destroying the sutures ends the restraint.

## Reactions

***Transfer Injury (3/Day).*** When Yselle takes damage while at least one conduit in the living chamber remains active, she can reduce that damage by 10, to a minimum of 0. A corresponding wound appears on Nera. This visible transfer does not require tracking Nera's hit points.

## Legendary Actions

Yselle can take 2 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Yselle regains spent legendary actions at the start of her turn.

***Surgical Step.*** Yselle moves up to half her speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

***Bone Scalpel (Costs 2 Actions).*** Yselle makes one Bone Scalpel attack.

**DM note:** Both Yselle portraits use this stat block. Her combat form is revealed when negotiations fail; The Unfolding at 85 hit points is the later scream, not a second hit point pool. Her challenge rating assumes an active living chamber. All her listed monster features are distinct from spells; she has no Spellcasting trait.

# Living Chamber and Ritual Targets

## The Living Chamber
On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), the chamber takes one of the following lair actions. It can't use the same lair action two rounds in a row. These are effects of the covenant itself: they continue if Yselle is incapacitated or defeated, provided at least one conduit remains active. They do not require Yselle to see a target.

## Pain Pulse
One creature within 30 feet of an active conduit must make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

## Grasping Flesh
One creature touching the chamber floor must succeed on a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw or be restrained by grasping tissue until initiative count 20 on the next round. A creature can use its action to make a DC 16 Strength (Athletics) check, freeing itself or a creature within its reach on a success.

## Three Living Conduits
Each conduit is a magical object with AC 15, 30 hit points, and immunity to poison and psychic damage. A conduit reduced to 0 hit points is disabled. As an action, a creature within 5 feet of a conduit can instead attempt a DC 16 Intelligence (Arcana), Wisdom (Medicine), or Intelligence (Religion) check to disable it. On a failed check, the creature takes 7 (2d6) necrotic damage and can try again on a later turn. A conduit disabled by damage or a successful check stays disabled until the covenant is resolved.

## Dispel Magic
As a specific exception for this adventure's magic, casting dispel magic on a conduit automatically disables that conduit for 1 minute. No ability check is required. It reactivates after that time if the covenant has not been resolved and it has not otherwise been disabled. The conduits are objects; a spell that targets only creatures can't target one unless its rules say otherwise.

## When All Three Are Disabled
The lair actions stop, existing Grasping Flesh restraints end, and Yselle loses Covenant Regeneration and access to Transfer Injury for as long as all three conduits are disabled. Her other features still function. Disabling the conduits alone does not break the pact or return injuries to the village. Nera's suspended heart becomes vulnerable: AC 13, 25 hit points, immunity to poison and psychic damage. Resolve the three endings using the adventure's original rules. Lower the ribcage toward the pit's rim so characters can reach the heart for the replacement-vessel ending; this movement occurs as the last conduit shuts down.

## Nera and the Listener
Nera is the captive at the center of the ritual. The Listener's combat presence is represented by the chamber and its lair actions; it is not a conventional target in the sky. Neither needs a separate creature stat block for this one-shot.

## Encounter Counts
Use these counts in place of the original level-5 counts. For a faster opening fight, use four attendants instead of five (3,600 adjusted XP, easy). The mirror encounter remains optional. All calculations use the 2014 encounter multipliers and include the listed supporting monsters.

## Five 8th-Level Characters
The party's 2014 encounter thresholds are 2,250 XP (easy), 4,500 XP (medium), 7,000 XP (hard), and 10,500 XP (deadly). The sixth bell still starts the original eight-round deadline; count from the party's entry into the final chamber. The conduit and ritual actions add pressure beyond an ordinary fight.

## Practical Use
Allow the short rest already offered by the module, with its one-bell cost. Start with the listed finale rather than adding more enemies for party size. Yselle's damage prevention and regeneration depend on the conduits, so make those connections evident. The Rejected fight can be bypassed with the reincorporation order and trolley as discussed; don't require killing them to proceed.

## Scope and Balance
These are new homebrew conversions for five 8th-level adventurers, written in 2014 5e stat-block conventions. Challenge ratings are design estimates, not playtest results. The four blocks cover the two original enemy types and the two Rejected variants added during preparation. NPCs, Mercy Marks, recruitment, the final sharing ritual, and the consequences of each ending retain their original rules unless stated above. The optional well encounter was a separate suggestion and is not part of this encounter set.
