# Isolde Mournsong

**Custom Lineage (Medium humanoid); Bard 7, College of Requiems; Entertainer background (PHB 2014).** Charm-based controller, party face and necromantic support.

A funeral singer who remembers the name of every person whose bones she has ever commanded. Isolde believes the dead deserve both dignity and a chance to defend the living. Bond: recover a stolen book of burial names. Flaw: treats a promise made at a graveside as unbreakable.

## Combat plan

1. Play the Doss lute for Hypnotic Pattern: DC 16 with disadvantage against susceptible creatures. Do not use this plan against charm immunity.
2. Use Polymorph for a badly injured ally or control a dangerous foe; use Healing Word for emergency recovery. Only one concentration spell can run at once.
3. Once actual remains and preparation time are available, Animate Dead supplies servants. Each inspiration bonus action can also grant Dirge dice to up to three and command them.

## Features and resources

**Custom Lineage and feat provenance** (TCE; PHB 2014). Lineage +2 Charisma plus racial Fey Touched +1 Charisma; Bard 4 adds +2 Charisma. House feat War Caster has no stat increase. Variable Trait is Perception proficiency, not darkvision.

**Fey Touched** (TCE; SCC). Choose Charisma, Misty Step and Silvery Barbs. Each spell has one free cast per long rest and can also use appropriate slots. These spells do not count against the ten bard spells known. Silvery Barbs is the official Strixhaven enchantment spell.

**War Caster** (PHB 2014). Advantage on Constitution saves to maintain spell concentration when taking damage; can perform somatic components with weapons/shield in hand; can replace a provoked opportunity attack with a one-action spell targeting only the provoking creature. Does not grant advantage on every Constitution save.

**Chilling Melody** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Two bonus necromancy cantrips count as bard spells: Toll the Dead and Chill Touch, using Charisma. These are additional to the three normal bard cantrips.

**Pluck the Heartstrings** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). An ally with your Bardic Inspiration can expend its die for extra necrotic weapon damage. A living creature with your die reduced to 0 HP may expend it as a reaction to be reduced to 1 HP instead. These are additional ways to spend the SAME die, not free extra dice.

**Stir the Bones** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Learn Animate Dead as an extra bard spell. When giving a Bardic Inspiration die, choose up to THREE controlled undead within 60 feet to each receive a Dirge die for 10 minutes, and issue mental commands as part of that bonus action. A Dirge die used to turn an attack into a hit can also add its result to damage. No undead or remains are assumed at character creation.

**Bardic Inspiration and recovery** (PHB 2014). Five d8 Bardic Inspiration uses per short/long rest from Charisma 20 and Font of Inspiration. Bonus action, target other than yourself within 60 feet who can hear you. A die lasts 10 minutes. Song of Rest adds 1d6 when eligible creatures spend Hit Dice during a short rest. Countercharm uses an action and the 2014 rules.

**Jack of All Trades and expertise** (PHB 2014). Enable Jack of All Trades after import: +1 to ability checks that do not already include proficiency, including initiative (total +3) and Dispel Magic checks (total +6). Expertise in Persuasion and Insight is already imported.

**Amulet of Health** (DMG 2014). Attuned; SETS Constitution to 19. Native Constitution is 8. Equipped HP 66; without the amulet maximum HP is 31 and Constitution modifier is -1. This is an item-dependent level-7 build, not a permanent stat increase.

**Doss Lute** (DMG 2014 p.176). Attuned bard instrument. Play while casting Hypnotic Pattern to impose disadvantage on its DC 16 Wisdom save. This applies to spells that actually impose the charmed condition and have S or M components; it does not apply to every enchantment, Suggestion or Silvery Barbs. Seven attached spells can each be cast from the lute once per dawn, without slots, using its action activation.

**Spell inventory** (PHB 2014). Ten normal bard spells known, plus Animate Dead from the subclass, two Fey Touched spells, three normal bard cantrips and two subclass cantrips. Seven item-only spell cards are NOT additional known spells and cannot be cast with slots unless independently learned. Slots 4/3/3/1; spell attacks +8 and spell DC 16.

**Magical Inspiration** (TCE p.27). Optional Tasha feature: an ally with your Bardic Inspiration die that casts a damage or healing spell can expend the die to add it to the damage or healing for ONE affected target. The same die cannot also be spent on another Inspiration benefit.

## Undead preparation

No corpses or undead are supplied for free. Once suitable remains and preparation time exist, one normal 3rd-level Animate Dead casting raises **one** servant or renews control over **up to four existing** servants. Control requires renewal every 24 hours. The starting purchases supply four shortbows, four quivers and 80 arrows; these are equipment for future servants, not four additional creatures.
