# Bryn Reedwhistle

**Custom Lineage (Medium humanoid); Circle of the Shepherd Druid 7; Hermit (PHB).** Summoner, healer and fear-resistant wilderness expert.

A marsh hermit who maps vanished roads and shelters frightened animals. Bryn carries a little bloodstone heart found in the ruins of a drowned village, treating its alien whispers as clues rather than commands. Bond: return the village's survivors to safe ground. Flaw: assumes every frightened creature can be soothed.

## Combat and utility plan

1. Conjure allies using a DM-selected legal beast option. Summon Bear Spirit afterward so chosen creatures in its initial aura receive 12 temporary HP.
2. Use Unicorn Spirit instead for group recovery: a slot Healing Word restores its normal amount and adds 7 HP to each chosen creature in the aura. Goodberry eating does not activate it.
3. Keep concentration protected, use terrain/cover, and avoid replacing Conjure Animals with another concentration spell unintentionally. Use the heart against fear and on important lore checks.

## Features and recovery

**Lineage and Skill Expert** (TCE). Lineage +2 Wisdom; racial Skill Expert +1 Wisdom, Stealth proficiency and Perception expertise. Variable Trait grants the Perception proficiency being doubled. Druid 4 adds +2 Wisdom. House War Caster has no stat increase. No racial darkvision.

**War Caster and amulet** (PHB 2014; DMG 2014). Advantage on concentration saves caused by damage. Amulet sets native Constitution 8 to 19: equipped HP 66, without it HP maximum 31. Equipped concentration is +4 with advantage; this is not advantage on every Constitution save.

**Telltale Heart** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Uncommon, attuned. While on your person, advantage on saves against becoming frightened. If frightened without any save, receive a DC 15 Wisdom save to avoid that condition. While HOLDING the stone, once per long rest gain advantage on one Intelligence ability check. These are item features, not bonuses to spell DC.

**Speech of the Woods** (XGE). Speak/read/write Sylvan and communicate with beasts through their noises and motions. This does not make beasts automatically friendly or able to discuss complex ideas.

**Spirit Totem** (XGE). Bonus action: place a spirit within 60 feet, with a 30-foot-radius aura. Lasts 1 minute or until incapacitated; one use per short/long rest. Move it up to 60 feet with another bonus action. It does not require concentration. Choose Bear, Hawk or Unicorn when summoned.

**Bear Spirit** (XGE). Creatures chosen in the aura WHEN IT APPEARS gain 12 temporary HP. Allies in the aura have advantage on Strength checks/saves. This is not a repeating Twilight Sanctuary-style refresh and does not stack temporary HP. Summon creatures before placing Bear if you want them to receive its initial HP.

**Unicorn and Hawk Spirits** (XGE). Unicorn: when you CAST a slot spell that restores HP, chosen creatures in the aura also regain 7 HP; helps detect creatures in the aura. Eating a Goodberry or drinking a potion does not trigger it. Hawk: use your reaction to give advantage to one attack against a target in the aura, and allies in the aura have Perception advantage.

**Mighty Summoner** (XGE). Beasts/fey summoned or created by your spells gain +2 HP per actual Hit Die, not per CR or character level. Natural weapons count as magical for overcoming resistance/immunity. Example: an ordinary wolf with 2 Hit Dice gains +4 HP, becoming 15 HP, before any Bear temporary HP. No bonus accuracy or automatic attack advantage is added.

**Conjure Animals choices** (PHB 2014). Use 2014 Conjure Animals and its group initiative, not the Carrion Raven maneuver. Choose a number/CR option; official guidance leaves actual species to the DM. Eight wolves are a request, not a guaranteed default. No animals start present and no concentration buff is pre-applied.

**Wild Shape / Wild Companion** (PHB 2014; TCE). Two Wild Shape uses per short/long rest. At Druid 7, normal shapes are CR 1/2 or less, no flying, and last up to 3 hours. You cannot cast spells in these forms. Optional Tasha Wild Companion spends a use to cast Find Familiar as an action without material; the familiar is fey and temporary, up to 3 hours. No familiar starts present.

**Spells and components** (PHB 2014; TCE). Three normal Druid cantrips and 12 prepared spells; no Shepherd bonus spell list. Tasha's optional expanded Druid list permits Revivify. Carry one consumed 300 gp diamond. Hide armor and shield are nonmetal. Druid rituals must be prepared.

**Discovery** (PHB 2014). Hermit background: knows that certain marsh paths follow ancient seasonal tide cycles, a story hook to develop with the GM. This grants no unlisted stat bonus, spell, item or feat.

**Wild Shape item handling** (PHB 2014; XGE). Mighty Summoner does not add HP to your own Wild Shape form. Use the beast form's physical statistics and normal Wild Shape rules. Do not assume the amulet continues setting Constitution to 19 if it merges into the form; whether equipment can be worn by a form depends on its anatomy.
