# Lio Glassmere

**Custom Lineage (Medium humanoid); School of Divination Wizard 7; Cloistered Scholar (SCAG).** Fast-acting fate controller and investigator.

A scholar who records dreams in the margins of borrowed books. Lio believes that knowing a person's secret wish can prevent a tragedy, but worries that intervention may create the fate they fear. Bond: return every borrowed book. Flaw: over-explains choices that others would leave to intuition.

## Combat and utility plan

1. Use +7 initiative to land Web, Hypnotic Pattern, Slow or Polymorph early. Use a LOW recorded Portent before an important enemy save, not after seeing its roll.
2. For investigation, a suitably low Portent can help the ring reveal a target's desire; the item only reveals information and does not compel behavior.
3. After expending slots, a slotted See Invisibility can recover a 1st-level slot through Expert Divination. Use Arcane Recovery separately once per day, up to five total slot levels.

## Features and recovery

**Lineage and feat sources** (TCE; PHB 2014). Lineage +2 Intelligence; racial Fey Touched +1 Intelligence; Wizard 4 +2 Intelligence. House Alert is its original 2014 version and grants no stat increase. Variable Trait Perception; no racial darkvision. Medium humanoid; use normal carrying capacity. Medium size allows Dimension Door to carry Medium or smaller companions.

**Alert** (PHB 2014). Initiative +5 beyond Dexterity, total +7. While conscious cannot be surprised. Unseen attackers do not gain attack advantage against you merely because you cannot see them. This does not let you see invisible creatures or target them with sight-requiring spells.

**Portent** (PHB 2014). After a long rest roll and record TWO d20s. Before an attack/check/save roll by yourself or a creature you can see, substitute one recorded result; at most once per turn. Each result is spent once, and unused results expire at the next long rest. Choose BEFORE the roll, not after seeing failure. Does not bypass Legendary Resistance.

**Expert Divination** (PHB 2014). Casting a divination spell of level 2 or higher WITH A SLOT recovers one expended slot of a lower level (never above 5). Prepared See Invisibility with a 2nd-level slot can recover a 1st-level slot. Ritual casting and the ring's item activation do not spend slots and do not trigger recovery.

**Ring of Hidden Desire** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Uncommon, attuned. Action, visible creature: DC 13 Wisdom save. On failure learn its darkest desire; no automatic friendship, truthfulness or obedience. On success that creature is immune to the ring for 24 hours. Intelligence 3 or less is unaffected. Three uses per day, restored at MIDNIGHT, not long rest. The grimoire does not raise this item's fixed DC.

**+2 Arcane Grimoire** (TCE p.120). Attuned, HOLD the grimoire for +2 to Wizard spell attacks/DC: +10 and DC 18. It can be focus and spellbook. Arcane Recovery restores FIVE total slot levels at level 7 rather than four, once per day after a short rest; e.g. a 4th-level and a 1st-level slot. It does not improve Counterspell ability checks or non-spell item DCs.

**Spellbook and preparation** (PHB 2014). Eighteen free class spellbook spells: six at Wizard 1, plus two each at Wizard 2-7. Twelve prepared each day from Intelligence 20 + Wizard 7. Fey Touched adds Misty Step and Silvery Barbs outside that count. Four Wizard cantrips. No paid extra spells are assumed.

**Rituals and components** (PHB 2014). Find Familiar, Detect Magic and Identify can be cast as rituals from the spellbook without preparation. Identify needs the carried reusable 100 gp pearl; each familiar casting consumes a 10 gp incense/charcoal/herb bundle, three carried. No familiar starts present. Normal new level-up spells do not cost gold to learn.

**Defenses and concentration** (PHB 2014). Unbuffed AC 12 and HP 37. Mage Armor costs a slot and raises AC to 15 for 8 hours; Shield reaction adds 5 temporarily. No War Caster, Resilient or Constitution save proficiency is granted. Concentration is +1; positioning, cover, Shield, Absorb Elements and a precommitted Portent can protect a key spell. Do not claim automatic concentration advantage.

**Fey Touched and spell limits** (TCE; SCC). Misty Step and Silvery Barbs each have one free use per long rest and can also use slots. Intelligence is their ability. The 2014 bonus-action spell rule applies to Misty Step; another spell that turn must be a one-action cantrip. One concentration spell at a time.

**Library Access** (SCAG). Cloistered Scholar background: access to your cloister's library and helpful connections at other libraries, subject to their normal rules. No feat or ability increase.

**Divination Savant and Cantrip Formulas** (PHB 2014; TCE). Divination Savant halves the gold and time needed to copy divination spells into your book: normally 25 gp and 1 hour per spell level instead of 50 gp and 2 hours. No paid copies are assumed here. Optional Tasha Cantrip Formulas allows replacing one known Wizard cantrip after a long rest.
