# Kesh Mossarrow

**Bugbear (Monsters of the Multiverse, 2022); Gloom Stalker Ranger 7; Folk Hero (PHB).** Ambush archer and camouflaged scout.

A quiet bugbear who rescued a village from its own lord's hunters and now keeps its forest paths safe. Kesh marks arrows with little carved leaves and dislikes being praised where everyone can hear. Bond: the families who hid him. Flaw: judges strangers by how quietly they move.

## Combat and utility plan

1. Act before your target to add bugbear damage to each hit; take three shots on the first Attack action and add the Dread Ambusher d8 only to its extra shot. Use Sharpshooter selectively.
2. Use darkness against darkvision-dependent enemies and camouflage against sight-based detection. Neither grants automatic surprise or hiding.
3. Use Pass without Trace to help the group approach, or Spike Growth for terrain control. These conflict with Favored Foe and Zephyr Strike concentration.

## Features and recovery

**Race and feat progression** (MPMM; TCE; PHB 2014). MPMM bugbear chooses +2 Dexterity and +1 Constitution. Ranger 4 Piercer adds +1 Dexterity. House Sharpshooter is the original 2014 feat with no stat increase. Final Dex 18, Con 14. No racial feat.

**Surprise Attack** (MPMM). MPMM bugbear: on an attack-roll hit, +2d6 damage if the target has not yet taken a turn in the current combat. Not limited to once per turn, and does not require the surprised condition. Stops applying to a target after it has taken its first turn.

**Bugbear traits** (MPMM). Darkvision 60 feet becomes 90 with Umbral Sight. Fey Ancestry gives advantage against charm. Long-Limbed adds 5 feet only to melee attacks on your turn, not bow range or off-turn reach. Powerful Build doubles ordinary carrying capacity. Sneaky grants Stealth and fitting in Small spaces without squeezing.

**Dread Ambusher** (XGE). Add Wisdom +2 to initiative for total +6. First combat turn: +10 feet walking speed and one extra weapon attack as part of the Attack action, with +1d8 damage on that additional attack if it hits. Normally two attacks; three on that first turn.

**Umbral Sight and Iron Mind** (XGE). In darkness, invisible to a creature relying on darkvision to see you there; not universal invisibility or automatic hiding. Darkvision 90 feet. Ranger 7 Iron Mind grants Wisdom saving throw proficiency, already included.

**Carvaloth Scale Armor** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Uncommon, no attunement. AC 14 + Dex capped at 2 = 16 without shield. Observers have disadvantage on Perception checks to SEE you. Its advantage on Stealth to hide FROM SIGHT cancels scale mail's normal Stealth disadvantage; such rolls are straight, not automatically advantaged. For other Stealth checks, the ordinary armor disadvantage still matters. Apply -5 to sight-based passive Perception where the disadvantage applies.

**Archery / Sharpshooter / Piercer** (PHB 2014). Bow attack +11, or +6 for optional -5/+10. Sharpshooter ignores half/three-quarters cover and long-range disadvantage, not total cover. Piercer permits one piercing damage-die reroll per turn and one extra weapon die on a piercing critical. Apply rerolls/extra critical dice manually.

**Deft Explorer and Roving** (TCE). Optional Tasha replacements: Canny doubles Stealth proficiency and adds Elvish/Dwarvish. Roving at 6 adds 5 feet to walking and grants equal climb/swim speeds. These replace Natural Explorer rather than stacking its benefits.

**Favored Foe** (TCE). Replaces Favored Enemy. On a hit, mark with no action; add 1d6 on the first hit against that target on each of your turns at Ranger 7. Three marks per long rest, each up to 1 minute with concentration. Cannot maintain with Pass without Trace, Spike Growth or Zephyr Strike. No extra Favored Enemy languages.

**Primal Awareness / focus** (TCE). Replaces Primeval Awareness: Speak with Animals and Beast Sense are additional known spells, each free once per long rest or cast with slots. Ranger does not gain general ritual casting. Optional Spellcasting Focus allows the purchased totem for ranger spells.

**Rustic Hospitality** (PHB 2014). Folk Hero background: common folk may provide modest shelter and help you hide or recover, unless doing so would expose them to danger. No automatic combat allies, feat or ability increase.
