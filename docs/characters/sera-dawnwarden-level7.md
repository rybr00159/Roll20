# Sera Dawnwarden

**Half-Elf (PHB 2014); Paladin 7, Oath of Zeal; Acolyte background (PHB 2014).** Shield-bearing protector and marked-target smite striker.

A former temple prosecutor who broke with corrupt elders after discovering their secret prison. Sera directs her zeal against coercive cults and their tyrants, not ordinary worshippers. Bond: free the remaining prisoners. Flaw: hears uncertainty as a challenge to her resolve.

## Combat plan

1. Bonus-action Mark of the Heretic, then take two staff attacks. If the target starts its turn within your weapon reach, use your reaction for a third attack.
2. On later turns, a PAM shaft attack can add another hit; select where to spend smites after seeing the hit or critical. Hunter's Mark is a separate concentration/bonus-action investment.
3. Stay within 10 feet of vulnerable allies for +4 saves and blindness/invisibility protection. Your reaction cannot simultaneously serve Heretic, PAM and another opportunity attack.

## Features and resources

**Half-elf advancement** (PHB 2014). Racial +2 Charisma, +1 Constitution, +1 Wisdom. Paladin 4 adds +1 Charisma and +1 Constitution: native Con 16, Cha 18, Wis 14. House feat Polearm Master grants no stat increase. Darkvision 60 feet; advantage on saves against charm; magic cannot put you to sleep.

**Belt and equipment dependency** (DMG 2014). Belt sets native Strength 8 to 21. With it, splint's Strength requirement is met and speed remains 30 feet. Without the belt, reduce Strength and apply the heavy-armor speed penalty. Attunement: one slot used.

**Dueling and Polearm Master** (PHB 2014). Wield +1 quarterstaff in ONE hand and a shield in the other. Dueling adds +2 damage; a shield is not a second weapon. Attack action makes two 1d6+8 attacks; qualifying bonus action makes one 1d4+8 shaft attack. PAM can also trigger an opportunity attack when a creature enters the staff's 5-foot reach.

**Mark of the Heretic** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Channel Divinity, bonus action: mark a visible creature within 30 feet for 1 minute. Your weapon attacks against it crit on 19 or 20. When ITS turn starts, you may use your reaction to make ONE weapon attack against it, provided normal weapon range/reach permits. Does not grant extra reactions, movement or advantage.

**Inquisitor's Eye** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Alternate Channel Divinity, bonus action: for 10 minutes gain advantage on Investigation, Insight and Perception checks and cannot be surprised. Shares the same one-per-rest Channel Divinity pool with Mark of the Heretic.

**Aura of Clarity** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). At Paladin 7, while conscious, you and friendly creatures within 10 feet cannot be blinded. Chosen creatures/objects in that range cannot benefit from invisibility. Does not reveal things outside the aura.

**Aura of Protection** (PHB 2014). While conscious, you and friendly creatures within 10 feet add Charisma +4 to saving throws, including your concentration saves. AFTER IMPORT add an enabled global +4 save modifier while conscious. Base displayed saves omit the aura. Disable it when unconscious; it does NOT add +4 to your own ordinary death saves.

**Divine Smite** (PHB 2014). After a melee weapon hit, optionally spend a slot: 2d8 radiant for 1st level, 3d8 for 2nd, plus 1d8 against undead/fiends. It is a feature, not a bonus-action spell in 2014. Dice double on critical hits. Do not add smite to every attack without spending slots.

**Paladin resources** (PHB 2014). Lay on Hands 35 HP per long rest; can spend 5 to cure one disease or neutralize one poison. Divine Sense 5 uses per long rest. Channel Divinity 1 per short/long rest. Divine Health grants disease immunity. Seven prepared paladin spells plus four always-prepared oath spells; slots 4/3.

**Hunter's Mark and action economy** (PHB 2014). The spell adds 1d6 to each weapon hit but requires concentration and its own bonus action to cast/move. Mark of the Heretic is a different, non-spell feature. You cannot cast Hunter's Mark and use the PAM bonus attack on the same turn. One reaction must be shared between Heretic attacks and opportunity attacks.

**Spellcasting hands** (PHB 2014). The shield can bear your holy symbol for spells using an unpriced nonconsumed material component. Without War Caster, stow the staff to provide a free hand for somatic-only spells such as Cure Wounds. Plan object interactions; do not assume the PAM house feat solves spell components.

**Harness Divine Power** (TCE p.52). Optional Tasha feature: bonus action, spend your one shared Channel Divinity use to recover an expended 1st- or 2nd-level spell slot. At Paladin 7 this feature is limited to twice per LONG rest, even if short rests refresh Channel Divinity. It competes with Mark of the Heretic for the shared resource.
