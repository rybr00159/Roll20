# Elin Voss

**Custom Lineage (Medium humanoid); Inquisition Domain Cleric 7; Sage (PHB).** Armored investigator and anti-spell protector.

An investigator who catalogs magical disasters and hunts the people exploiting them. Elin distrusts unchecked power but judges spellcasters by their actions. Bond: finish a dead colleague's investigation. Flaw: struggles to stop working when a mystery puts someone in danger.

## Combat and utility plan

1. Use bonus-action Spell Shield before a dangerous spell exchange; its protection depends on retaining its particular temporary HP. It is a feature, so an action spell can be cast on the same turn.
2. Against a concentrating caster, the weapon Strike adds 2d8 force and may yield temporary HP if concentration breaks. Against ordinary groups, Spirit Guardians is more efficient.
3. Save the reaction for the most important Rebuke or War Caster opportunity spell. Rebuke deals damage; it is not an automatic Counterspell.

## Features and recovery

**Lineage and feat sources** (TCE; PHB 2014). Lineage +2 Wisdom; racial Fey Touched +1 Wisdom, choosing Misty Step and Dissonant Whispers. Cleric 4 +2 Wisdom. House feat War Caster has no stat increase. Variable Trait grants Perception, not darkvision.

**War Caster** (PHB 2014). Advantage on Constitution saves to maintain spell concentration when damage is taken. Somatic components can be performed with weapons/shield held. Can replace a provoked opportunity attack with a one-action spell targeting only that creature. Does not waive priced/consumed material handling.

**Fey Touched** (TCE). Misty Step and Dissonant Whispers each have one free cast per long rest and can also use slots. Wisdom is their ability; the amulet applies to the save DC of these spells. These are separate from the 12 cleric preparations.

**Inquisition armor and weapons** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). The Player Pack subclass grants martial weapons and heavy armor at Cleric 1. Gauntlets set native Strength 8 to 19, meeting splint's requirement; without them reduce Strength and walking speed appropriately.

**Witch Hunter's Strike** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). On a weapon hit, expend a use for +1d8 force, or +2d8 force if the target is concentrating on a spell. Five uses per long rest. If this damage causes a failed concentration save, gain temporary HP equal to the extra force dealt. This is not automatic extra damage on every hit.

**Channel Divinity: Spell Shield** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Bonus action, visible target within 30 feet: 1d10+7 temporary HP, lasting up to an hour. While ANY of these particular temporary HP remain, target has resistance to spell damage and advantage on saves against spells. Losing/replacing them ends those riders. Different temporary-HP pools do not stack.

**Rebuke Invoker** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Reaction when a visible creature within 60 feet casts: Constitution save, taking 1d8 per level of slot expended +5 force on failure, half on success; cantrips count as level 1. Five uses per long rest. The available Player Pack entry omits the save DC: this build conservatively assumes feature DC 16 (8+PB+Wis), pending the GM's ruling. Do not automatically add the amulet's spell-only DC bonus. This feature does not automatically counter the spell.

**Channel Divinity and Harness Divine Power** (PHB 2014; TCE). Two shared Channel Divinity uses per short/long rest; amulet grants one additional activation per dawn without spending a use. Turn Undead/Destroy Undead CR 1/2 follow the 2014 rules. Optional Tasha Harness Divine Power: bonus action, spend a use to regain an expended 1st/2nd-level slot, at most twice per long rest.

**Casting totals and hands** (PHB 2014). Wisdom 20 plus +2 amulet: spell DC 18, spell attacks +10. No item bonus to damage/healing or Dispel Magic checks. War Caster handles somatic components with shield/weapon; stow the weapon to handle a pearl or consumed diamond. No extra attack or Divine Strike at Cleric 7.

**Equipment attunement** (DMG 2014; TCE). Amulet and gauntlets require attunement, two of three slots used. Both bonuses are already included in the snapshot. Native Strength without the gauntlets is 8.

**Researcher** (PHB 2014). Sage background: when you do not know a piece of lore, you often know which person or source might provide it. Access may still require travel, negotiation or a dangerous expedition. No feat or ability increase.
