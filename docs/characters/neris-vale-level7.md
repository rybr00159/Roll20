# Neris Vale

**Custom Lineage (Medium humanoid); Rogue 7, Misfortune Bringer; Charlatan background (PHB 2014).** Accurate ranged rogue, scout, face and enemy-save disruptor.

A soft-spoken document forger whose mismatched eyes seem to notice bad luck before it happens. Neris sells false identities to people fleeing cruel masters and makes the masters pay for the service. Bond: protect the people living under those new names. Flaw: trusts an elaborate plan more than a sincere promise.

## Combat plan

1. Mark a durable target with Evil Eye, or skip marking when an ally already enables Sneak Attack and use the bonus action for a second shot.
2. After the target fails the mark save, Curse of the Ruined can force a worse save against an ally's decisive spell. Doomed can instead rescue your own missed attack.
3. Use one hand crossbow with a free loading hand. Neither Crossbow Expert nor the mark grants extra reactions, automatic hits, or Sneak Attack more than once on a turn.

## Features and resources

**Custom Lineage and feats** (TCE; PHB 2014). Lineage +2 Dex and racial Piercer +1 Dex, then Rogue 4 +2 Dex: final Dexterity 20. House feat Crossbow Expert has no stat increase. Variable Trait grants darkvision 60 feet. Native Constitution remains 13; no unearned +1 is added.

**Crossbow Expert** (PHB 2014). Ignore loading on proficient crossbows and no ranged-attack disadvantage merely from enemies within 5 feet. After the Attack action with this one-handed hand crossbow, bonus action can fire the SAME hand crossbow again. Keep the other hand FREE to load ammunition; no shield or second hand crossbow.

**Piercer** (TCE). Once per turn on a piercing hit, reroll one damage die and use the new result; may choose an eligible piercing Sneak Attack die. A piercing critical adds one extra weapon damage die, beyond normal critical doubling. These bonuses are manual; the base attack row does not pre-apply a reroll.

**Sneak Attack and defenses** (PHB 2014). Sneak Attack 4d6 ONCE PER TURN, not once per hit. Normally needs advantage or an eligible ally within 5 feet, with no disadvantage. Uncanny Dodge uses reaction to halve damage from a visible attacker's hit. Evasion: on Dexterity saves for half damage, take none on success and half on failure. Cunning Action uses the same bonus action as Evil Eye or the crossbow follow-up.

**Evil Eye** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Bonus action: visible creature within 60 feet makes a DC 13 Charisma save or is marked for up to 1 minute, ending when a different target is marked. No jinx-point cost and no concentration. Against the marked target, Sneak Attack does not need advantage or an adjacent ally, but still fails if you have disadvantage and remains once per turn.

**Misfortunist** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Four jinx points per SHORT or long rest; two misfortunes known. Choose Curse of the Ruined and Curse of the Doomed. Replace one known misfortune after each long rest. DC 13 is Charisma-based, not Dexterity-based.

**Curse of the Ruined — 2 jinx** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Reaction after the marked creature rolls a saving throw, before success is declared: force a reroll and use the LOWER result. A strong setup for an ally's control spell. Does not bypass Legendary Resistance.

**Curse of the Doomed — 1 jinx** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). After missing the marked target with a weapon attack, spend your reaction to make an additional weapon attack as part of the same action. It can rescue a missed Sneak Attack, but does not grant a second Sneak Attack on the same turn. Competes with Uncanny Dodge and Curse of the Ruined for your one reaction.

**Expertise** (PHB 2014). Four choices total: Stealth and Perception at Rogue 1, Persuasion and thieves' tools at Rogue 6. Skill expertise is imported; tools expertise is a manual Dexterity +11 check. Thieves' Cant is separate from normal languages.

**Cloak of Protection** (DMG 2014). Attuned. +1 AC already included. AFTER IMPORT add an enabled global +1 saving throw modifier, including death saves. Final Str/Dex/Con/Int/Wis/Cha saves are +0/+9/+2/+4/+2/+3. Do not add the modifier twice.

**Steady Aim** (TCE p.62). Optional Tasha feature: if you have not moved this turn, use a bonus action to gain advantage on your next attack this turn, then your speed is 0 until turn end. This uses the same bonus action as Evil Eye, Cunning Action or the Crossbow Expert follow-up; it is not free advantage on both shots.
