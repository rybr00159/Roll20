# Mara Ironwake

**Custom Lineage (Medium humanoid); Fighter 7, Blade Breaker; Soldier background (PHB 2014).** Flying polearm striker and stun controller.

A former siege sergeant who collects broken enemy weapons and writes letters for soldiers who cannot read. Mara fights to make sure the next retreat brings everyone home. Bond: her old company. Flaw: takes command before asking whether anyone wants a commander.

## Combat plan

1. Use Iron Punisher with GWM against targets you can afford to expose yourself to. On one advantaged hit, Helmsplitter forces a disadvantaged Constitution save against stun.
2. Action Surge doubles the action attacks, not bonus actions. Save Refusal to Bleed or Wall of Blades for a reaction on the enemy turn.
3. Switch to Steel Serpent when reach is more valuable than reciprocal advantage; its extra reach applies only on your turn. Use boots to reach flying enemies.

## Features and resources

**Custom Lineage and feats** (TCE; PHB 2014). +2 Strength and racial Slasher +1 Strength; both already included. Choose darkvision 60 feet. House feat Great Weapon Master has no stat increase; Fighter 4 grants Polearm Master; Fighter 6 grants +2 Strength.

**Great Weapon Master / Polearm Master** (PHB 2014). Two glaive attacks with the Attack action. If that action attacks only with the glaive, one bonus-action shaft attack deals 1d4+7 bludgeoning. Choose -5 to hit/+10 damage separately on each heavy glaive attack, including the shaft. A critical/kill can instead enable a full glaive bonus attack. You still have only ONE bonus action. PAM also allows an opportunity attack when an enemy enters your 10-foot reach.

**Slasher** (TCE). Once per turn a slashing hit can reduce speed by 10 feet until your next turn. A slashing critical makes the target attack with disadvantage until your next turn. The bludgeoning shaft does not trigger Slasher.

**Martial Maneuvers** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). 14 maneuver points per LONG rest; four known maneuvers. DC 16 from Strength; the +2 weapon does not raise this DC. At most one maneuver PER TURN, even with Action Surge. Reactions on another creature's turn can use a different maneuver.

**Stance: Iron Punisher** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Enter a known stance when initiative is rolled, or change with a bonus action. Iron Punisher gives advantage on your melee weapon attacks, but ALL attacks against you also have advantage. It is a deliberate risk, not free advantage.

**Stance: Steel Serpent** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Your second stance, learned at Fighter 7, increases your reach by 5 feet only ON YOUR TURN. It replaces Iron Punisher. It does not increase off-turn opportunity-attack reach at this level.

**Helmsplitter — 4 points** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). On a hit, force a DC 16 Constitution save or stun the creature until the end of its next turn. If the triggering attack had advantage, the target saves with disadvantage. Normal attack damage is retained.

**Refusal to Bleed — 1 point** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Reaction when taking damage: reduce it by 1d10+7. For 3 additional points, if caused by an attack, the same reduction applies to all damage from that attacker until your next turn.

**Arcing Iron — 1 point per extra target** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Bonus action to empower the next melee attack this turn with additional attacks against other creatures within reach, one point per extra creature. Competes with the PAM bonus attack; each additional target is a different creature.

**Wall of Blades — 2 points** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Reaction when hit by a weapon attack: make a weapon attack against the attacker and optionally use that attack-roll total as your AC until your next turn, including the triggering attack. Do not use GWM's penalty when you want the largest defensive result.

**Unyielding Determination** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Advantage on saving throws made DURING YOUR OWN TURN. Does not grant advantage on every save during enemy turns.

**Fighter resources** (PHB 2014). Action Surge once per short/long rest grants one extra action, not a second bonus action or reaction. Second Wind heals 1d10+7 as a bonus action once per short/long rest. Defense adds +1 AC while armored. No Indomitable before Fighter 9.

**Winged Boots** (DMG 2014). Attuned; flying speed 30 feet, up to 4 hours stored. Each use spends at least 1 minute. Regain 2 hours for every 12 hours not used. This is item flight, not concentration.
