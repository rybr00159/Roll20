# Torren Ravenmantle

**Mountain Dwarf (Tasha customized origin); Barbarian 7, Path of the Carrion Raven; Outlander background (PHB 2014).** Raging heavy-weapon attacker and beast summoner.

A dwarf exiled for sharing a winter food cache with outsiders. Torren carries carved ravens for companions who never returned, and treats hungry animals more gently than cruel people. Bond: the outcast families of the high passes. Flaw: hoards food long after the danger has passed.

## Combat plan

1. For a major fight, action Call of the Beast Lord for a Giant Ape, then bonus-action rage and use the Feral Celerity attack. Maneuver concentration is specifically legal during rage.
2. On later turns take two reckless glaive attacks, often with GWM. The ape takes its own immediately following turn.
3. Pack Leader can generate an off-turn attack, but uses your reaction and requires the enemy to be within 5 feet. Only one reaction is available before your next turn.

## Features and resources

**Mountain dwarf advancement** (PHB 2014; TCE). Use Tasha's optional origin rules to move the two +2 increases to Dexterity and Constitution: Dex 13 to 15, Con 15 to 17. Barbarian 4 adds +1 Dex and +1 Con, reaching Dex 16 / Con 18. No racial feat. House feat GWM has no stat increase.

**Belt and unarmored defense** (DMG 2014; PHB 2014). Attuned belt SETS Strength to 21; native Strength is 8, not 21 permanently. AC is unarmored 10 + Dex 3 + Con 4 = 17, without a shield. Remove the item bonuses if separated from the belt.

**Rage / Reckless Attack / Extra Attack** (PHB 2014). Four rages per long rest; +2 melee damage on Strength attacks and resistance to bludgeoning, piercing and slashing while raging. Two attacks with the Attack action. Reckless Attack gives advantage to Strength melee attacks on your turn, while attacks against you gain advantage until your next turn. Rage ends under the 2014 duration/attack/damage rules.

**Feral Celerity** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Entering rage with a bonus action also allows ONE weapon attack as part of that same bonus action. Carrion Raven explicitly allows concentration on MANEUVERS during rage; it does not allow concentrating on spells.

**Martial Maneuvers** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). 14 points per long rest, four known maneuvers, DC 15 based on Constitution 18. At most one maneuver per turn. Chosen: Call of the Beast Lord, Axolotl's Regeneration, Pack Leader, Predator's Howl.

**Call of the Beast Lord — 5 points** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Action: conjure a friendly beast of CR no greater than your level within 60 feet. At level 7, a Giant Ape (CR 7) qualifies under the printed Player Pack text. It acts immediately after your turn on the same initiative; verbal commands require no action. Lasts 10 minutes, until 0 HP, or until maneuver concentration ends. One concentration effect at a time. This is not the lower-CR self-polymorph maneuver.

**Axolotl's Regeneration — 2 or 6 points** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Bonus action: regain 1d12+4 HP. Spending 6 points also regrows one lost limb or organ. Competes with rage activation and other bonus actions.

**Pack Leader — 1 point** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Reaction when an enemy within FIVE feet of you is attacked: make one melee attack against it, and give the triggering attack advantage. A glaive's 10-foot reach does not remove the 5-foot trigger requirement.

**Predator's Howl — 2 points** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Bonus action: two chosen creatures within 30 feet make DC 15 Wisdom saves or are frightened until your next turn. While frightened by this effect, each time weapon attacks damage them they take an additional 1d6 psychic.

**Raven's Spirit** (Grim Hollow: Player Pack). Animal Handling proficiency gained at Barbarian 6. Can cast Speak with Animals as a ritual; this grants no spell slots and cannot be cast during rage.

**Feral Instinct and Danger Sense** (PHB 2014). Advantage on initiative from Feral Instinct. Can act normally when surprised if you enter rage before anything else on the first turn. Advantage on Dexterity saves against effects you can see while not blinded, deafened or incapacitated. Fast Movement adds 10 feet while not wearing heavy armor.

**Dwarven traits** (PHB 2014). Darkvision 60 feet, poison resistance and advantage on saves against poison. Stonecunning doubles proficiency for Intelligence (History) checks about stonework, even if not ordinarily proficient in History. Brewer's supplies proficiency.

**Primal Knowledge and Instinctive Pounce** (TCE p.24). Optional Tasha features selected: Barbarian 3 grants Nature proficiency; Barbarian 7 permits movement up to half your speed as part of entering rage. This can accompany the Carrion Raven attack in the same rage bonus action; it does not grant a second bonus action.

## Summoned beast reference

Call of the Beast Lord permits a beast of CR up to the character's level. At 7 this includes the original Monster Manual **Giant Ape, CR 7**: AC 12, HP 157, speed/climb 40 feet; two fist attacks at +9 for 3d10+6 each, or one rock at +9 for 7d6+6 (50/100 feet). It is Huge, which may prevent useful positioning in small spaces. It acts immediately after Torren and obeys verbal commands without an action.

[Optional Giant Ape import template](../../examples/companions/npc-giant-ape-torren.json). Importing that reference does not mean a beast is already summoned. The maneuver costs **5 points**, lasts up to **10 minutes**, and consumes maneuver concentration; at 14 points/day you can afford two such summons with four points left. Do not use the separate CR-half-level restriction from Bestial Transformation for this different maneuver.
